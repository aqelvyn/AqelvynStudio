## 1. Executive Summary

AQELVYN ($AQEL) is the native utility token of **AQELVYN Studio**, a
production-grade prompt studio containing **615+ master build-prompts** and
**100 free Web3 build prompts** — blueprints that rebuild every major app,
open-source repo, YouTube channel, and brand with built-in AI and native Web3,
plus a growing library of DeFi, DEX, GameFi, SocialFi, launchpad, RWA
tokenization, NFT, wallet, and infrastructure prompts.

$AQEL turns a content product into a **self-reinforcing economy**: the token is
used to unlock prompts, mint prompt NFTs, run the AI enhancer, govern the
treasury, and reward the community that shills and grows the brand. Every paid
action in the studio burns or buys back $AQEL, creating sustained demand that
scales with usage.

The long-term vision is to evolve from a token project into a **large-scale Web3
corporation** — a content marketplace, an AI tooling company, and a community
economy — with $AQEL as the equity of that corporation.

## 2. The Problem

1. **Prompts are fragmented and low-quality.** Millions of people search for
   "best AI prompts" and "web3 app builder" and get scattered, shallow lists —
   not complete, deployable blueprints.
2. **Building web3 apps is hard.** Developers and founders must combine AI,
   smart contracts, tokenomics, and security — a steep, expensive barrier.
3. **Communities have no economic reason to promote.** Projects pay agencies to
   shill; there is no mechanism where holders are *directly* rewarded for
   growing the brand.
4. **Content is not owned.** Creators can't own, monetize, or transfer the
   prompts and blueprints they build.

## 3. The Solution — AQELVYN Studio (the Utility)

AQELVYN Studio is the product $AQEL powers. It has four pillars:

| Pillar | What it does | $AQEL role |
|---|---|---|
| **Prompt Library** | 615+ master build-prompts (apps, repos, YouTube, brands) | Unlock prompts with $AQEL; holders get discounts/free tiers |
| **Free Web3 Kit** | 100 free web3 build-prompts (DeFi, DEX, GameFi, SocialFi, launchpads, RWA, NFTs, wallets, infra) | Funnel → converts free users into token holders |

Today the studio operates a 1 CRO-per-prompt / 100 CRO unlock-all paywall with
on-chain verification. $AQEL extends this into a full token economy (see §6).

  inflation) and to access higher tiers (including the FlipSuit role).

## 6. Utility — How $AQEL Powers the Studio

$AQEL is **required** to participate in the premium economy:

1. **Unlock prompts** — pay $AQEL (or CRO, with $AQEL discounted) to unlock any
   master build-prompt. Burn 20% of each fee.
2. **Mint prompt NFTs** — turn any prompt into a tradeable NFT; minting costs
   $AQEL and embeds royalties for the creator.
3. **AI Enhancer & Generator** — each run costs $AQEL; a share is burned, a
   share goes to the treasury.
4. **Hold-to-unlock tiers** — holding thresholds grant automatic perks:

| Tier | $AQEL held | Perks |
|---|---|---|
| **Wanderer** | 0+ | Browse, free Web3 Kit |
| **Builder** | 1,000+ | 10% discount on all unlocks |
| **Architect** | 10,000+ | 25% discount + early access to new prompts |
| **FlipSuit** | 100,000+ | Free unlocks + revenue-share + governance + shill rewards (see §8) |

5. **Revenue share** — FlipSuit and Architect holders receive a pro-rata share
   of studio revenue (paid in CRO or $AQEL).
6. **Governance** — $AQEL holders vote on treasury allocation, new prompt
   categories, fee parameters, and roadmap priorities.

## Appendix A — Token Contract (reference interface)

```solidity
// SPDX-License-Identifier: MIT
pragma solidity ^0.8.20;

import "@openzeppelin/contracts/token/ERC20/ERC20.sol";
import "@openzeppelin/contracts/access/Ownable.sol";

contract AQEL is ERC20, Ownable {
    uint256 public constant MAX_SUPPLY = 100_000_000 * 10**18;

    constructor(address treasury) ERC20("AQELVYN", "AQEL") Ownable(treasury) {
        _mint(treasury, MAX_SUPPLY);
    }

    // Burn mechanism (callable by the studio's fee router).
    function burn(uint256 amount) external onlyOwner {
        _burn(msg.sender, amount);
    }
}
```

*Note: the production contract adds vesting, staking, and fee-split modules.
The treasury wallet is supplied as a constructor argument — no address is ever
hard-coded, and the deploy script refuses to deploy with an unset wallet.*
